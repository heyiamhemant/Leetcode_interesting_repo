# Bakeries of India — Verified Item Inventory

A per‑bakery, per‑item inventory of items sold at the requested bakeries across 10 Indian cities, with a source URL on every dot. Compiled from official websites, Zomato, Swiggy, Magicpin, EazyDiner and reputable press (Telegraph India, The Hindu, Times of India, Indian Express, Condé Nast Traveller India, LBB, Mint Lounge, etc.).

## Accuracy notes (read once)

- **Per‑item citations are the rule**, not the exception. Where multiple items come from the same source page (e.g. a Zomato menu URL), the same URL is cited per item — that is deliberate.
- **No fabrication.** Where a bakery has no verifiable online menu, it is marked *Status: No verifiable online menu found* with searched URLs.
- **Spelling preserved as printed** at the source (Zomato, Magicpin, and even some official sites contain typos — they are reproduced verbatim, e.g. "Chocoloate", "Vailla", "Browine", "Samos", "Past", "weffers", "namkins").
- **Aggregators often hide full SKU lists in the mobile app**; where Zomato/Swiggy only exposed category headers, the category and item count are still cited but individual SKUs in that bucket are not invented.
- **Section labels** in this document follow the user's source list ordering.
